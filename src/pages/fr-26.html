About the retreat

Worship the Father, seek Jesus, encounter the Holy Spirit.

Fall Retreat is an event for university students to worship the Father, seek Jesus, and encounter the Holy Spirit.

FAQs

I’ve never been to a retreat before. What can I expect?

Sessions include live worship, teaching, and a time for prayer. Devotional times are an opportunity for students to spend time with the Lord on their own with live instrumentals. During small groups, students will discuss topics relevant to their faith with students from other campuses.

What time is pickup and dropoff at my campus?

The pickup and drop off details are emailed to all registered students closer to the retreat date. Pickup/drop off times vary based on the distance of your campus from the retreat location. Our transportation may depart from campuses as early as the morning of Friday, October 16, and may return to campuses as late as the afternoon/evening of Sunday, October 18. Attendees should plan their schedules accordingly.

Where is the retreat located?

Fall Retreat 2026 will be hosted at Courtyard New Haven Orange/Milford (136 Marsh Hill Rd, Orange, CT 06477). All sessions, meals, and overnight accommodations will be at the hotel.

Who is the retreat hosted by?

This retreat is one of several student retreats held during the academic year by Alabaster Group, a student-focused church based in New York City and Boston. To learn more about us, visit our about us page.

I am a student, but I don’t attend one of the listed universities. What should I do?

Please email {{site.email}}, and a Registration Coordinator will respond with more information.

Can I attend the retreat if I am not a Christian?

Yes, we welcome students of all backgrounds.

Do meals include options for dietary restrictions?

We will offer vegetarian options when possible. Students with more restrictive diets may prefer to provide their own food.

I have another question. Who should I reach out to?

For all other questions/concerns, please email {{site.email}}, and one of our Registration Coordinators will respond.
