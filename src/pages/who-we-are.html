About · Since 2011

Who we are

Alabaster Group exists to glorify Jesus Christ and to work towards the expansion of the influence of his gospel in every place where our members live and work.

The ministry was established by the Holy Spirit through our founder in 2011 to be a ministry that prioritized genuine love for, pursuit of, and relationship with God himself.

We believe that authentic faith in Jesus by a community of believers is evidenced by the operation of God’s power (1 Cor 4:20) and the fruit of his work in our lives. Thus, we aim to show, by good works, by demonstrations of God’s power, and by his love, the greatness of God’s glory and the truth of his gospel.

Today, our work focus is centered around several different communities: university campuses and companies which employ our members. We invite you to visit us at any of our Sunday services or at a special service or other event. Additionally, if you are a university student, please join us on campus or at a student retreat.
